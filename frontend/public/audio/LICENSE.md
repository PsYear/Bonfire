# Campfire recording

`campfire-crackle.mp3` is derived from a real campfire recording:

- Title: **Campfire Close Crackling Sticks.mp3**
- Author: **FunWithSound**
- Source: https://freesound.org/people/FunWithSound/sounds/588401/
- Downloaded preview: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/588/588401_6456158-hq.mp3
- License: **CC0 1.0 Universal** — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Processing: selected a close crackling section, reduced low-frequency rumble,
emphasized short wood transients, balanced peaks, and crossfaded the loop boundary.
The bundled stereo MP3 is approximately 44 seconds long.
